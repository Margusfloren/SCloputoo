# KPIs

This folder documents the management-reporting metrics for the pellet industry in two development stages: **V1 (completed thesis)** and **Full Version / V2 (planned further development)**.

## 1. V1 – completed management view

V1 focuses on assessing actual sales and production results, budgets and deviations.

**Sales KPIs**
- Sales Actual, t
- Sales Budget, t
- Sales Variance (Actual − Budget), t
- Sales Attainment (Actual / Budget), %

**Production KPIs**
- Production Actual, t
- Production Budget, t
- Production Variance (Actual − Budget), t
- Production Attainment (Actual / Budget), %

**Production and sales comparison**
- Production vs Sales (Production Actual − Sales Actual), t
- Production vs Sales (Production Actual / Sales Actual), %

The metrics can be analysed by period, plant and product. **The sales-channel selection affects the sales and sales-budget measures.** Production is not allocated to channels; the production–sales balance comparison on the V1 Dashboard and QA Validation pages uses **sales from all sales channels**, even when the user has selected a specific channel.

**OTIF is not part of the V1 functionality.** On-time and in-full order fulfilment is planned for the next development stage.

[View the V1 KPIs](./01_V1_KPI-d/)

## 2. Full Version / V2 – planned development

The aim of V2 is to extend performance reporting towards supply chain planning and exception management.

Planned topics include, for example:
- sales forecasting, Budget vs Forecast vs Actual, forecast snapshots and Forecast Accuracy / Bias;
- finished-goods inventory, coverage in days, min/target/max levels and warehouse capacity;
- production capacity utilisation and bottlenecks;
- raw-material requirements, BOM, moisture and yield calculations;
- more detailed sales-mix and channel analysis;
- **initial implementation and further development of OTIF**;
- a management view focused on deviations.

These are **plans, not KPIs already implemented in V1**.
